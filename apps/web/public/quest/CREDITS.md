# Snow Quest art credits

All of the art in this folder was made for this project.

- `rooms/*.jpg`: the nine painted rooms (and `rink-open.jpg`, the rink with the trail cleared), generated with an AI image
  model from our own descriptions.
- `heroes.png`, `players.png`: the townspeople and the playable characters, generated the same way and cut into sprite
  sheets by script.
- `art/`: the title and ending illustrations, dialogue portraits, the terminal, the keycap trophy, the gate barrier, and
  the character-picker portraits, generated the same way.
- `rooms/*.walk.png`, `*.fg.png`, `*.fg2.png`, `*.depth.json`: collision masks and cut-out layers made from the room
  paintings by script, so characters can walk behind trees, lamps, and fences.

No third-party art packs are used.
